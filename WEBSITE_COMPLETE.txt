📝 FORM BACKEND SETUP EXAMPLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Using Formspree (FREE):

1. Go to https://formspree.io
2. Sign up and create a new form
3. Copy your form endpoint
4. In each HTML file, update the form tag:

   CHANGE FROM:
   <form class="form" onsubmit="return handleContactForm(event)">

   CHANGE TO:
   <form action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST" class="form">

5. Forms will now email you submissions!
